Round 17 confirmed fixed: labels filled through a slot (native and aria-labelledby).

Finding (confirmed in the code): the label reader followed a shadow host's shadow root AND its own
children, and glued every text node together. A component whose slot shows the host's "OTP" read
"OTPOTP", so \botp\b missed it; the field stayed unmarked and, on a clean page, its value was reported.
The same gluing made "Enter" "OTP" in two tags read "EnterOTP".

Fix (owner layer: composedText, the one reader of label words): a label is read twice — as the page draws
it (a shadow host through its shadow root only, a slot through its assigned nodes or its fallback) and as
its markup holds it (a child no slot shows included, per fieldWords' "shown or hidden"). Each reading is
taken run together ("Pass" "word" -> Password) and apart ("Enter" "OTP"), and no reading runs into
another. The words feed only the password/payment classifiers; nothing else reads them.

Guard (site-36): an OTP label held in markup that no slot shows. It passes on fd03b15 and after the fix,
and fails on the drawn-only variant (the minimal fix the review proposed) — shown below.
